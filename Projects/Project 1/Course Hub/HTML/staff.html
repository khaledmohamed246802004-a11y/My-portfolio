CSD341 · COURSE SUPPORT

Course Staff and Student Support

This page explains the teaching and support responsibilities relevant to the course. The supplied lecture PDFs do not provide a verified staff directory, individual contact details, or office-hour timetable, so none are invented here.

Teaching Roles

Course Instructor

The course instructor presents the main concepts, explains software engineering terminology and methods, and provides guidance about the course requirements and assessments.

Practical and Laboratory Support

Practical support helps students work through exercises, discuss project decisions, and apply the lecture concepts to examples and team activities.

What Can You Ask About?

  • Software quality attributes and types of software products.
  • Software processes, process flows, and process models.
  • XP, Kanban, Scrum, and the Agile tools introduced in the lectures.
  • Functional and non-functional requirements, user stories, and requirements validation.
  • How to interpret an exercise or prepare a project deliverable.

How to Ask for Help

  1. Identify the lecture number and topic related to your question.
  2. Explain what you understood and where you became unsure.
  3. Include a short example or the relevant part of your work when appropriate.
  4. Do not include passwords, private account details, or confidential information.

For the official contact channel and office hours, follow the information announced by the course instructor or university platform.
